The Lifecycle of the Monarch Butterfly

Monarch butterflies are famous for their incredible long-distance migration. Every autumn, millions of these insects fly south toward warmer climates. They rely heavily on milkweed plants to lay their eggs and feed their larvae. The caterpillars grow quickly before forming a chrysalis. After metamorphosis, a beautiful adult emerges to repeat the cycle.

Scientists tracking this migration pattern once discovered a uniquely massive roosting site inside a protected forest in Wyoming. This spectacular annual phenomenon continues to fascinate biologists worldwide.
